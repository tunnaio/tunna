# Code of conduct

This project's spaces, the issue tracker, pull requests, discussions and
reviews, are for working on tunna. Everyone taking part is expected to
make that possible for everyone else.

## Expected

- Be direct about the work and considerate of the people. Critique code,
  designs and decisions freely; do not attack the person who made them.
- Assume competence and good faith. Ask before concluding.
- Keep disagreements on the record: an issue that names the decision and
  the reasoning beats a thread of asides.
- Respect that the maintainer decides what goes in, and that "no" with a
  reason is a complete answer.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or demeaning comments of any kind, including
  about a person's identity, background, or experience.
- Publishing someone's private information, or contacting them outside
  the project's spaces about a project matter without their consent.
- Sustained disruption: derailing threads, repeating a rejected proposal
  without new information, or pressuring people to respond.
- Any of the above in another space when it is aimed at someone because
  of their participation here.

## Enforcement

The maintainer enforces this. Reports go to the maintainer privately,
through the contact on their GitHub profile; a report that involves a
security matter can also use the repository's private vulnerability
reporting. Reports are read, and the reporter hears back. Responses range
from a private word to removal from the project's spaces, at the
maintainer's judgement, in proportion to what happened.

## Scope

Applies to every space the project runs, and to anyone taking part in
them, the maintainer included.
